# Retention, individuals' rights and breaches

#### When to use this file

Read this when a feature creates data that must eventually be deleted, when building export or deletion, or when planning for incidents.

## Retention
- Every table with personal data has a retention rule (stale requests, declined invites, leavers) and a deletion or anonymisation job.
- Backups, logs and copies in other systems are covered too.

## Individuals' rights
Access (one month to respond; the clock pauses while you wait for clarification you've asked for), rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawing consent.
- Provide one written channel for requests.
- Provide an in-app deletion request that notifies the church's data protection contact.
- Provide per-person export and delete covering all linked records.

## Breaches
- A personal data breach (e.g. an access-rule gap): assess it first. Report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware, unless it's unlikely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms. If the risk to people is high, tell them without undue delay.
- As processor, tell the church without undue delay so it can decide.
- Have a breach procedure before you need one.

## Checks
- [ ] Retention rule and job exist for new data
- [ ] Export and erasure cover the new data
- [ ] Breach procedure names who decides and who tells the church
