# People, sign-up, consent and communications

#### When to use this file

Read this when a feature adds people to the system (sign-up, invites, referrals, leader-added, imports, syncs), asks for consent, or sends emails, texts or push notifications.

## People who haven't signed up
- Someone added by a friend, leader or import hasn't consented. Don't create a full user account for them. Keep a minimal record (name, one contact method for the invite), link it to an account only when they accept, and delete it if they don't within a set period.
- Don't mark them as a member or infer belief until they accept.
- Record the origin of every person's data (self sign-up, referral, leader-added, sync, import) and every change since, with who made it.

## Sign-up and privacy notices
- Every form (sign-up, join request, website embed) shows the privacy notice: who the controller is, what's collected, why, who sees it, how long, and their rights, with an explicit opt-in.
- Send a confirmation email that also serves as a record.
- Pick and record a lawful basis per purpose: legitimate interests or contract for running groups; consent for optional things (photos visible to others, marketing).

## Consent
- Opt-in (no pre-ticked boxes), granular, stored with timestamp and wording, and as easy to withdraw as to give.
- Support sending consent requests to existing people and parents.

## Communications (PECR)
- Service messages are fine; promotional messages need consent or valid soft opt-in. A church that is a charity can also use the charitable purposes soft opt-in (from 5 February 2026), but only for contact details collected on or after that date.
- Every email has unsubscribe and a preferences page. Push notifications used for promotion follow the same rules.

## Checks
- [ ] Invited or imported people have a minimal record only, with an expiry
- [ ] Data origin and change history recorded
- [ ] Notice shown and consent stored with wording and time
- [ ] Withdrawal is one step
- [ ] Promotional messages have a valid basis and unsubscribe
